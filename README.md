# Blazeit Raw: one-knob loudness, clip & heat

![Blazeit Raw free one-knob loudness, clip & heat plugin UI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/BlazeitRaw/main/blazeitraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**Instant loudness, clip and heat in one knob.**

Blazeit Raw is the free one-knob version of Blazeit. Turn a single BLAZE knob to add saturation and clip-heat, louder and hotter, kept level-matched so you only hear it get bolder, never a volume jump.

**macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): AU, VST3, CLAP, AAX, Standalone. Signed and notarized by Apple.

**Windows** 10 and 11, 64-bit: VST3, CLAP, Standalone. Authenticode signed.

AAX ships on macOS only.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install

Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/BlazeitRaw/releases/latest) and pick your platform.

**macOS**
1. Download **`BlazeitRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
2. Double-click it and follow the installer. It is signed and notarized by Apple, so it installs cleanly with no security warnings.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Blazeit Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

**Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit**
1. Download **`BlazeitRaw_Installer.exe`**.
2. Run it and follow the installer. It is Authenticode signed.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Blazeit Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

No dongle and no extra account on either platform.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **BLAZE** knob. Turn it up to add saturation and clip-heat in a single move. Your track gets louder and hotter, kept level-matched automatically so you only hear it get bolder, never a jump in volume. Works on drums, bass, synths, and the whole mix.

---

## 💻 System Requirements

**macOS**

- Apple Silicon or Intel Mac (Universal Binary)
- An AU, VST3, CLAP or AAX host

**Windows**
- Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit
- A VST3 or CLAP host
